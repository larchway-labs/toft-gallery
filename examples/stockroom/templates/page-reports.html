Send it on a schedule

The schedule is a job, declared in this app's toft.toml and nowhere else: this same binary, run as toft-app report in a process of its own, every Monday at 07:00 London time.

Job
report
Schedule
0 7 * * 1 · Europe/London
Parameters
report = inventory | low_stock | movements channel = slack | email | both to = ops@example.com

Change the schedule or its defaults by editing toft.toml and deploying. Run it now from the app's Automation tab, with toft job run report --param channel=both, or here:

A run's log, its deadline and whether it failed are on the Automation tab, and the Activity tab lists every run. The job does not share this process's scratch space. Without a writable storage mount it reads a freshly seeded register — the right numbers for a demo, and not the edits made here — and what it sends is listed in its run rather than in the table beside this.
