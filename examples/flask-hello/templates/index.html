The one that catches everybody

x_prefix=1. Toft's proxy strips /apps/{slug} before forwarding, so this process is mounted at /. A link it emits has to carry the prefix back, and ProxyFix reading X-Forwarded-Prefix is what makes url_for() do that.
A Location: header must not carry it. The proxy re-adds the prefix on the way out, so a redirect that includes it lands on /apps/slug/apps/slug. Links carry the prefix; redirects do not. That asymmetry is the single most common way a working app breaks on a platform.
