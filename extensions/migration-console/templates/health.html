What this app can reach

It holds two kinds of credential and nothing else. On this install, a read-scoped deploy key: it lists apps and groups, reads deployment history and a dependency verdict, and downloads a release — and it cannot deploy anything, including to this app. On each target, a deploy-scoped key minted there by an administrator of that install.

It never acts as you. Your role tells it what you may do; your identity, where an administrator has switched it on, tells it what to write on the record.
