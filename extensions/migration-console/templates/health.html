Migrations run in the background: process-migrations, a Toft job declared in this app’s toft.toml, runs every five minutes. It starts approved migrations that are due, follows their builds on the target and re-tests targets. Approving a migration to run now starts it straight away. Its logs and pause are on this app’s Automation tab. Not recorded means Toft has no runs to report yet, not that the job is missing.

What this app can reach

The console holds two kinds of credential. On this install, a read-scoped deploy key that lists apps and groups, reads deployment history and dependency verdicts, and downloads releases. It can’t deploy anything. On each target, a deploy-scoped key minted there by an administrator of that install.

It never acts as you. Your role decides what you may do here, and your identity is what the history records.
